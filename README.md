# Awesome MotiveWave Studies [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of awesome MotiveWave studies, indicators, and resources for technical analysis and algorithmic trading.

[MotiveWave](https://www.motivewave.com/) is a professional trading platform that supports custom studies written in Java. This list aims to collect the best community-created studies, tutorials, and resources.

## Studies

### Indicators

Custom indicators for technical analysis.

- [Volume Profile](https://github.com/MotiveWave/motivewave-extensions) - Volume analysis tools.
- [Advanced Moving Averages](https://github.com/MotiveWave/motivewave-extensions) - Enhanced moving average calculations.
- [WyckFlow.com Free Studies](https://github.com/zenart/wyckflow-free-studies) - Cumulative Volume Delta (CVD) with swing/immediate divergence markers and Bid/Ask Volume studies compatible with MotiveWave Community/Standard editions.

### Strategies

Automated trading strategies and systems.

- [Trend Following Strategy](https://github.com/MotiveWave/motivewave-extensions) - Basic trend following system.
- [Mean Reversion Strategy](https://github.com/MotiveWave/motivewave-extensions) - Mean reversion trading system.

### Drawing Tools

Custom drawing and annotation tools.

- [Fibonacci Extensions](https://github.com/MotiveWave/motivewave-extensions) - Advanced Fibonacci tools.
- [Pattern Recognition](https://github.com/MotiveWave/motivewave-extensions) - Automated chart pattern detection.

### Scanners

Market scanners and screeners.

- [Custom Market Scanner](https://github.com/MotiveWave/motivewave-extensions) - Configurable market scanning tools.
